After the Passing of Makoto, S.E.E.S are trapped in a time loop and a mysterious dungeon known as the abyss of time has appeared beneath the Iwatodai Dorm. S.E.E.S make their way beneath the abyss and at the bottom they encounter the manifestation of S.E.E.S guilt: A Shadow version of Makoto. It's up to Aigis to confront her nightmare.
